How to play

Two teams, two bases. Take the enemy flag from the top of their bunker and bring it to your own flag while yours is at home. First to the capture limit wins.

Moving

  • Jets (right mouse or E) lift you and push you the way you are moving. They burn energy, which comes back when you stop.
  • Skiing (hold Space on the ground): no friction. Go down hills to build speed, then jet over the top of the next one. This is how you cross the map fast.
  • Tap Space to jump. Landing hard hurts, so land on a downhill slope or jet just before you touch down.
  • A disc at your own feet throws you into the air. It costs some health.

Your base

  • Inventory stations inside the bunker give you your loadout (I): armor, weapons, a pack, and refill everything.
  • The generator in the middle of the bunker powers the stations and the turrets on the roof. If the enemy blows it up, fix it with a repair pack.
  • Heavy armor is slow but tough and carries the mortar. Light armor is fast and carries the laser rifle.

Ski courses

  • Pick a course on the front page. Ski through the gates in order: the lit one is next, the yellow one after that. The first gate starts the clock, the last stops it.
  • Shoot the targets (bullseyes on posts) on the way. Each one you miss adds seconds to your time. Discs are easiest: the splash counts.
  • Everyone runs in light armor with the same kit, so times compare. K starts the run over. Pacer bots run the course too; beat them.
