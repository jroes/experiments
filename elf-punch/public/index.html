Elf Castle
Wood Puncher
5000

Punch trees for wood. Eat mushrooms. Keep a fire lit at night: goblins come out, and the cold gets you. Other elves are in the same world.
Move: WASD or left thumb. Punch: space. Dodge roll: shift. Block: hold B. A block tapped just before a goblin swings parries it and stuns it; then punch for a crit. Hammer: E (or the HAMMER button) to build on a grid: 1-7 picks a piece, aim with the mouse or tap the ground, click or space places. Rotate camera: Z / C, right-drag, or the arrow buttons. Bag: I. Punch rocks for stone, craft tools in the bag. Quick wall: Q. Quick fire: F.
