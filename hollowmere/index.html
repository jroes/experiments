The Crown of Hollowmere

Walk with the arrow keys or by clicking. Type commands like take rope or give coin to crow, or use the buttons. Some things here can kill you. You can always try again from where you were.

How to play

  • Click or tap a thing to look at it. Pick a button (Take, Open, Use...) first to do that to the next thing you tap.
  • Use and Give take two things: tap what you carry, then what to use it on.
  • Walk off the edge of the picture to go to the next place.
  • Type save or use the Save button to keep a copy. The game also saves itself as you play.

Inspired by the old Sierra adventures. Original story, code and art. The picture is pixel art lit in code; if it runs slowly, turn Effects off.
