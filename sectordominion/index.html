    How to play

    You run a small star empire. Grow it, then take over the sector. Win by wiping out all three rival empires or by holding 70% of all worlds.

    • Tap or click a world to select it. Drag the map to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom, and Fit brings the whole map back.
    • You only see worlds near your worlds and fleets. Send scouts to look around.
    • Build ships at your worlds. They show up next turn. Ships cost credits.
    • Dispatch: pick ship counts in the world panel, hit Choose destination, then pick a world on the map.
    • A Transport sent to an empty world colonizes it. Natives have defenses and need an invasion.
    • To take a defended world, kill its garrison and defenses, then land more troops than it has. Each Transport carries 5 troops.
    • Fortify adds permanent defense to a world. Research makes ships stronger and income higher.
    • Play with friends gives you one link per friend. They open it and they are in. Turns play when everyone has sent orders, so nobody has to be online at the same time.
    • On a computer: E ends the turn, Esc cancels targeting.

    Inspired by classic 4X space strategy games. Original code and art.

    New game

    Play with friends

    Pick the settings and you get one link per friend. They open it and they are in, with nothing to type. Nobody has to be online at the same time: the game waits for each player's orders, plays the turn once everyone is in, and you can come back from any device with your link. Your game lives under a long random name that only your links contain. There are no accounts, so only send links to people you trust.

    Got an invite you can't open as a link?
