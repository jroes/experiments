    How to play

    You run a small star empire. Grow it, then take over the sector. Win by wiping out all three rival empires or by holding 70% of all worlds.

    • Click a world to select it. Drag the map to pan, scroll to zoom.
    • You only see worlds near your worlds and fleets. Send scouts to look around.
    • Build ships at your worlds. They show up next turn. Ships cost credits.
    • Dispatch: pick ship counts in the world panel, hit Choose destination, click a world on the map.
    • A Transport sent to an empty world colonizes it. Natives have defenses and need an invasion.
    • To take a defended world, kill its garrison and defenses, then land more troops than it has. Each Transport carries 5 troops.
    • Fortify adds permanent defense to a world. Research makes ships stronger and income higher.
    • Keys: E end turn, Esc cancel targeting.

    Inspired by classic 4X space strategy games. Original code and art.

    New game

    Multiplayer

    Play with up to 3 friends, directly browser to browser. No server. One person hosts and sends each friend an invite code. Empires nobody takes are run by the AI. Everyone ends their turn, then the turn plays out.

    Friends-only trust: the host's browser sends everyone the full game state, so anyone who opens dev tools could peek at hidden info.
