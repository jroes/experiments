    How to play

    You run a small star empire. Grow it, then take over the sector. You win by wiping out all three rival empires, or by holding 70% of all worlds. You lose if your empire is wiped out.

    Your first turns
    1. You start with one world (your capital, the green dot) and some credits. Credits come in every turn from your worlds.
    2. Tap your world, then in Build buy a Transport and a Scout. Ships show up in the garrison next turn.
    3. Press End turn (Send orders in a game with friends).
    4. Under Dispatch fleet, pick the Transport (and Scout), press Choose destination and tap an empty world (one with no red ring). When the Transport arrives, it settles the world and becomes yours.
    5. Spend credits on Research and more ships, and keep expanding. Rival empires do the same, so build warships before you meet them.
    Ships: what each one is for

    Speed is how many map units a ship covers each turn. The map is 120 by 80 units, and a fleet moves at the speed of its slowest ship. Research makes every ship you own hit harder and last longer.

    Taking worlds
    • Empty world (no red ring): send a Transport. It colonizes it. A fleet with no Transport just looks around and goes home.
    • Natives (red ring) and enemy worlds have defenses and troops. Send warships first: they fight the garrison and the world's defense. If the defenders are all dead, your Transports land.
    • Each Transport lands 5 troops and is used up. You take the world if you land more troops than it has (see Troops in the world panel). Bring a few extra.
    • If you do not kill all defenders, the fight is a stalemate and your fleet retreats. Check Defense and the garrison before you attack, and send more than you think you need.
    • Fortify adds permanent defense to a world of yours. Use it on border worlds.
    Worlds, income and tech
    • A world earns credits from its population, times its type: Capital x1.5, Industrial x1.3, Mining x1.2, Agricultural x1.0 (and grows fastest), Research x0.9 (and makes Research cheaper for you), Barren x0.6.
    • Research raises your tech level (up to 6). Each level adds income and makes your ships stronger. It gets more expensive each time.
    • You only see worlds near your own worlds and fleets. Grey and dim means last seen, so it may have changed. Scouts see far and are cheap, so send a few.
    Reading the map
    • Colour is the owner: green is you, grey is nobody. A red ring means natives, who fight back.
    • The faint circle around your worlds is how far you can see. Triangles are fleets, with a dashed line to where they are headed.
    • A white tick above a world means ships are stationed there.
    • Tap or click a world to select it. Drag the map to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom, and Fit brings the whole map back.
    Friends, and other devices
    • Games lists every game on this device. Start as many as you want, solo or with friends, and switch between them. It says which ones are waiting on you.
    • Play with friends gives you one link per friend. They open it and they are in. Turns play when everyone has sent orders, so nobody has to be online at the same time.
    • Play on another device (bottom of the side panel) gives you a QR code and a private link to carry your game to a phone or another computer.
    • On a computer: E ends the turn, Esc cancels targeting.

    Your games

    You can be in as many games as you like, solo and with friends. Each one is saved on this device on its own.

    Play with friends

    Pick the settings and you get one link per friend. They open it and they are in, with nothing to type. Nobody has to be online at the same time: the game waits for each player's orders, plays the turn once everyone is in, and you can come back from any device with your link. Your game lives under a long random name that only your links contain. There are no accounts, so only send links to people you trust.

    Got an invite you can't open as a link?

    What should we call you?

    Friends see this next to your empire when you play together. It is saved on this device, and you can change it any time.

    Play on another device

    Your game is ready

    Send each friend their own link. When they open it, they are in. There is nothing for them to type.

    You can start playing now. The game waits for your friends and plays each turn once everyone has sent orders. Each link is for one seat, so send it only to that friend. You can get these links again with Invite links in the game panel.
