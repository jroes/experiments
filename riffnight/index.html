      How to play

      Pick a song and a part. Notes slide down toward the line at the bottom. Hit them as they cross it.

      • Guitar, bass, drums by tapping. Tap the lane when its note reaches the line. Hold your finger down on long notes. On a keyboard use A S D F G.
      • Real guitar or bass. The phone listens through the mic. Each string has a lane and the number is the fret. Chords show their name. Tune up first with the Tuner. Headphones keep the song out of the mic, or play it quietly.
      • Vocals. Sing into the phone. The bars are the notes, your voice is the arrow. Any octave counts, so sing where it is comfortable. Each line gets a rating.

      Hit notes in a row to raise your multiplier. Miss and your part goes quiet, and the crowd meter on the left drops. If it runs out you are out of the song.

      Boost. Glowing notes fill the blue bar on the right when you hit all of them. Once it is half full, press Boost (or Space) for double points. In a band, boosting also brings back anyone who dropped out.

      Band. One person starts a band and sends the code or link. Everyone picks a part on their own phone and the leader starts the song. All phones start together. Same room: tick "Same room" so each phone plays only its own part. Anyone with the code can join, so only share it with friends. Scores come from each phone and are not checked.

      Timing. If notes feel early or late, run the tap test in Settings. For the mic, run the mic test without headphones.

      All songs, words and sounds are original and made in the browser. Inspired by the band games where you play along with plastic instruments.
