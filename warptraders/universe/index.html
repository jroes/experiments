      Join the universe

      One galaxy, shared with everyone who plays. Ports run low when people buy from them. You can run into other traders, and they can shoot you.

      There are no accounts. This browser keeps a secret key that is you. Lose it and the trader is gone. New traders can't be attacked for a few hours.

      How the universe works

      • The server runs the game. Everyone is in the same galaxy and the same ports.
      • Turns: you get one every 90 seconds, up to 600. They keep coming while you're away. A warp costs 1. Trading is free.
      • Ports sell or buy fuel ore, organics and equipment. When someone buys a port out, the price goes up for everybody. It slowly refills (hours).
      • Tap a sector on the map to fly there along sectors you already know.
      • Stardock (the gold one in the middle) sells holds and fighters. Nobody fights there.
      • If another trader is in your sector you can attack them (3 turns, needs fighters). The bigger fleet usually wins. The loser is sent back to Stardock with no fighters or cargo, and the winner takes what fits in their holds. New and just-beaten traders are protected for a while.
      • The leaderboard ranks net worth: credits, cargo, fighters and extra holds.

      Friends and strangers share this. Don't use a name or message you wouldn't say out loud.
