Invite friends

Send each friend their own link. It puts them in their seat. Anyone who has a link can play that seat and can see and change this game, so keep links to friends. Games are kept for 60 days after the last move.

How to play

Two to four tanks on a hill. Take turns lobbing shells at each other. Last tank standing wins the round. Most money after the last round wins.

  • Angle: 90 is straight up, 0 is right, 180 is left. Power: how hard the shell leaves the barrel.
  • Wind pushes shells sideways. The number at the top is how hard, and which way.
  • Shells dig holes. Tanks fall when the ground goes from under them, and that hurts.
  • Drag on the picture to aim from your tank, or use the buttons. Keys: ←→ angle, ↑↓ power, Tab weapon, Space fire. Hold Shift for bigger steps.
  • Online: pick Play online with friends in New game, then send each friend their link. Each person plays on their own screen, and turns go in order. If a friend goes quiet, you can let the computer play their turn.
  • Between rounds, spend money on bigger shells, a shield, or dirt to bury someone.
  • You can mix people and computers on one screen. Pass the device around.
